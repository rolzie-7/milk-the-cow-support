Privacy Policy

Milk the Cow · Last updated 23 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Milk the Cow ("the app", "we") collects and how it is used. Milk the Cow is designed to be private by default: the app works fully offline and does not require an account.

1. Information we collect

If you do not sign in, all of your data (sessions, stats, streak, settings) stays on your device. We do not collect or receive it.

If you choose to create an account and sign in, we collect and store the following so we can sync your data and enable social features:

  • Account information: your email address (used for sign-in and account recovery).
  • Activity data: your sessions, including duration, motion count, frequency, heart rate readings taken during a session, XP, and streak.
  • Profile: your chosen username and friend code.
  • Social data: the friends you add and friend requests you send or receive.

2. How we use your information

  • To sync your progress across your iPhone and Apple Watch.
  • To provide friends, friend requests, and leaderboards.
  • To operate, maintain, and improve the app.

3. How your information is shared

  • With friends you approve: when you accept a friend, your username, streak, XP, and session-derived scores may be visible to that friend on leaderboards. This is limited to friends you have accepted.
  • Service provider: account and synced data are stored using Supabase, which hosts our database and authentication. Data is protected with row-level security so users can only access their own data and data explicitly shared with them.
  • We do not sell your data, show third-party ads, or use third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

4. Data retention and deletion

Your local data remains on your device until you delete the app. If you have an account and would like your account and all associated data permanently deleted, contact us using the button below and we will remove it.

5. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored with access controls that restrict each account to its own data and data shared with approved friends.

6. Children's privacy

Milk the Cow is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Email us, or visit our Support page.
