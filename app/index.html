Milk it every day.

Milk the Cow logs each session from your Apple Watch, keeps your daily streak going, and ranks you against your friends.

Coming soon to theApp Store

For iPhone and Apple Watch. Free, no account needed.

One tap on your wrist starts a session.

Start from your Apple Watch and leave your phone where it is. When you finish, the watch shows what it recorded.

Duration
How long the session ran, to the second.
Motion count
Every motion your watch picked up.
Frequency
Motions per second across the session.
Heart rate
Read live from the watch while you go.

Sessions sync to your iPhone on their own, and the app works fully offline.

A session a day keeps the streak.

Every session earns XP. Hit your daily goal and your streak grows. You set the goal yourself on the Profile screen, so the bar is as high as you want it.

Friends make it a contest.

See who holds the longest streak, then take each other on in Frequency, Edge and Time challenges. Adding a friend takes three steps.

  1. Find your friend code on the Profile screen.
  2. Open Friends and tap the plus button.
  3. Enter your friend's code.

Start your streak today.

Free to use, with no account required. Sign in only if you want to sync between devices and add friends.
