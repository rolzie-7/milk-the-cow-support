FAQ

Do I need to create an account?

No. The app works fully offline without signing in. Signing in is optional and only used to sync your data across devices and unlock friends and leaderboards.

Does my data sync between iPhone and Apple Watch?

Yes. When you're signed in, your sessions, streak, and stats sync automatically across your devices. Your data is always kept on-device too, so nothing is lost offline.

How do I add a friend?

Open the Friends tab, tap the + button, and enter your friend's code. You can find and share your own code on the Profile screen.

How do I delete my account or data?

Email us at the address above and we'll remove your account and all associated data.
